5. Colour streak & reversal test

Add at least one result to detect a colour streak.

For independent roulette spins, a previous streak does not make the opposite colour “due”. With a European wheel, the chance of seeing the opposite colour at least once in the next 2 spins is 73.6%, and in the next 3 spins is 86.5%, when the target is one of the 18 opposite-colour pockets. Actual history is descriptive, not a reliable prediction.
